About Our Project

Our Community Service Project focuses on understanding local needs and creating positive social change through teamwork, awareness campaigns, and volunteer activities. This website documents our objectives, activities, progress, and achievements.

Project Objectives

Environmental Awareness

Encourage cleanliness, waste management, and environmental protection.

Community Education

Promote awareness about education, hygiene, and healthy habits.

Social Responsibility

Develop teamwork, leadership, and a spirit of service among students.

Our Beneficiaries

Local Residents

Community awareness and cleaner surroundings.

School Students

Educational support and awareness activities.

Public Community

Information about hygiene, safety, and health.
